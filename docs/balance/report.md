## What the data can't tell yet

- **Real wins.** 100 of 100 matches ended undecided at the cap, so results are mostly the net-worth proxy. Bot AI is making matches end (W5); Phase B runs full-length matches.
- **Healing, shields and crowd control per ability.** `heal.applied` and statuses carry no ability id, so support and control abilities show only casts. Abilities with no damage are listed as "utility: value not measured".
- **Item value at this sample size.** Most item scores are still the designer prior: the posterior moves away from it only where an item was held often. See the settled-pick and split-half rows above.
- **Situational picks and enemy-dependent value** (vs healers, vs divers): the model has no enemy-flock terms yet.
- **Ability win-rate effects and ablations** (D-EP-043): need paired runs with an ability disabled (Stage 5).
- **Heroes not yet implemented** (no games): they keep the designer prior.
- **Bot competence:** bots cast by use rules; "rarely cast" can mean the bot rarely finds a use, not that the ability is weak.
