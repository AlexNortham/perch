# Bots

Choose **Play** and you are one of six birds on the Dawn flock. The other eleven places, five allies and all six enemies, are filled by bots. Each bot plays one of the 24 birds and follows the same rules you do: it moves, aims, shoots, casts, buys and upgrades by pressing the same buttons, and it only reacts to what its flock can see.

## What a bot does

A bot picks what to do again and again through the match.

- **Lane:** walk with the fledglings, last-hit them, shoot seed pods to secure or deny them, and poke the enemy. It also shoots Twig Nests close to its lane.
- **Fight:** take on enemy birds it can beat, strafing and using its abilities.
- **Retreat:** back off to heal when its health is low.
- **Shop:** go back to a shop when it has the Seeds, buy the next item of a build suited to its bird, and spend Ability Points.
- **Farm:** clear Thickets near its lane when there is no wave to escort. Tier 1 camps first, tougher ones as it levels up.
- **Objectives:** go for the Golden Egg (one bot takes it with melee and the rest escort), Feather runes and Phoenix Embers with a heavy melee hit, and the Roc once enough allies are alive and the enemy is thin on the ground. See [[units/objectives|Objectives and the jungle]].
- **Push and siege:** from 20:00, or once it holds Rekindle, or once enemies are dead, the flock groups on one lane and takes down towers, then the Shrines, then the Patron.
- **Defend:** answer an attack on its own base, and answer your pings.

Bots find their way through the buildings and Cellar Run, and ride Updrafts.

## Limits

- All bots play at one difficulty (normal). There is no difficulty setting yet.
- Their builds come from a starter table the game's designer wrote. A learned set of builds is planned, so bots of the same bird may buy alike for now.
- Matches against them end: in a test of 20 seeded all-bot matches, every one finished with a winner, in about 35 to 55 minutes. You can concede from 15:00 in the pause menu.
