# Controls

All of these are the defaults. Every one can be changed on the [[getting-started/options|Options]] screen; binding a key that is already in use swaps the two actions.

{{controls-table}}

## Crouch, slide and Ctrl

Crouching while moving starts a slide. In Chrome and Edge, entering a match also enters fullscreen and locks the keyboard, which lets the game use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> as the crouch key even while you hold <kbd>W</kbd>: normally a browser would close the tab on Ctrl+W. In Firefox the keyboard lock does not exist, so <kbd>C</kbd> is the crouch key there. Esc pauses the game; hold Esc to leave fullscreen (a browser rule).

Options notes which key combinations only work in fullscreen on Chrome and Edge.

## Mouse and melee

- Left mouse fires; hold to keep firing.
- Right mouse aims, which zooms the view and tightens the spread.
- Tap the melee key for a light swing, hold it for a heavy one. The parry key blocks a melee hit and stuns the attacker. See [[mechanics/combat|Combat]].
- Middle mouse pings: a tap is a contextual ping and holding opens a wheel.

## Moving

Movement is described on [[mechanics/movement|Movement]]: run, sprint, jump, dash, dash-jump, slide, mantle, Updrafts, Chimneys and Vines.

## Upgrading abilities

Hold the upgrade modifier (Left Alt by default). Aiming pauses and a cursor appears over the HUD. Click an ability icon, or press its key (1 to 4), to unlock or upgrade it when you can afford it. Hover an icon to read what its next tier does and what it costs. Let go and you are aiming again at once. There is no separate menu: see [[mechanics/economy|Economy]] for what Ability Points are.

## There is no Interact key

Nothing in the game needs one.

- **Updrafts:** hold <kbd>Space</kbd> (Jump) within a few metres of the cable. A quick tap is only a jump. See [[mechanics/movement|Movement]].
- **The Golden Egg:** hit it with melee to pick it up. Pressing melee while carrying it sets it down. Delivery is automatic.
- **Feather runes, Phoenix Embers and Woven Nests:** a heavy melee hit (hold the melee key).
- **Berries, Hides, Lookouts, Bough Springs and the Cellar Run:** walk in or stand on them.
- **Shops:** the shop key opens the Hoard when you are in a shop zone.

## The scoreboard

The scoreboard is always on, as a bar at the top of the screen, so it has no key. The timer is in the middle with each flock's total Seeds beside it, and each flock's six birds are shown with health bars, Seeds and respawn countdowns. Enemies your flock cannot currently see are dimmed. A strip beneath shows both Patrons' health and phase, the towers still standing in each lane and when the next Egg is due.
