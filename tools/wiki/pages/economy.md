# Economy

**Seeds** are the only resource. Every Seed you earn adds to your **net worth**, which never goes down and sets your level, and to your **wallet**, which buys items.

{{economy-core}}

## Seed pods

When a fledgling dies, a seed pod pops out. Shooting the pod while it is ripe resolves it. A hero on the side that killed the fledgling **secures** it for the full pod share. A hero on the fledgling's own side can **deny** it: a smaller reward, and the enemy gets nothing. Abilities do not resolve pods; guns and melee do. A pod that is not hit withers.

## Last hits

## Levels

Net worth sets your level, up to a maximum. Each level-up is a **Molt**. The ultimate unlocks at a set level, and a few levels give two Ability Points instead of one.

{{levels-table}}

## Molts

Every Molt raises the bird's base stats: health, gun damage and Song power, with each bird's own growth listed on its [[heroes/index|hero page]]. A level-up also heals you by the health you gained.

## Ability Points

Ability Points (AP) buy ability upgrade tiers, not items. Each ability has three tiers bought in order, anywhere and at any time, even while dead. Unspent AP carries over. Hold the upgrade modifier to open the panel; see the [[heroes/abilities|Abilities]] list.

## Bounties

Killing a hero pays a bounty shared by the killer and the assisters. It grows with the victim's net worth and their kill streak, and gets a comeback multiplier when the victim's flock is richer than yours.

{{bounty-table}}

## Objective rewards

Destroying a structure pays every hero of your flock.

{{objective-bounties}}

## Flex slots

The first kills of each structure type also unlock **flex slots**: extra item slots shared by the whole flock for the rest of the match, shown in the last column above. See [[items/index|The Magpie's Hoard]].

## Respawn

Heroes respawn at the Home Roost with full health. Cooldowns keep running while you are dead.
