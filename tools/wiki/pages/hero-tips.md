# Hero tips

## shrike
Lay the Thorn Larder where enemies will walk, not where they stand. Combine the Briar Patch with a root to hold a lane. Barbs travel, so lead moving targets.

## albatross
Open fights with the Wing Slam or Tempest Dive, then hold the Gale Shield between your flock and the enemy guns. The shotgun is strongest up close.

## kingfisher
Use Glint to start fights unseen, then spend your gun on whoever the ambush bonus hit. Riverstrike is a finisher, not an escape.

## lyrebird
Keep Mended Song for the ally taking the most damage. Echo Double draws shots away from you. Cast Discord on a group of enemy shooters, then back off.

## owl
Your range is your safety. Use Night Eyes to find the enemy flock and Hush to stop an enemy ability before it lands. Aim carefully: the Owl's head shots are the strongest in the game.

## pelican
Gulp a fledgling to refill health, or a hero to remove them from the fight. Switch on Feeding Frenzy as your gun starts running dry and fire in short bursts at range.

## secretarybird
Kick, dash and stamp in sequence. Each move feeds the next, so chain them rather than using them alone. Keep a stamina pip for escaping.

## starling
Fire Murmuration down a lane to hit a whole group. Scatter turns you into a swarm that cannot be shot, which makes it a fine escape and an even better entrance.

## hornbill
Mark your target with Marked Prey and focus them. Heavy Rounds slow, so open with them when you want a kill. Bounding Hop refills half the magazine mid-fight.

## flamingo
Place the Pink Pool where your flock will stand, not where it is now. Poise protects a carry from a stun, so cast it on the bird the enemy will go for.

## penguin
Toboggan bowls through groups and lets you cross open ground fast. Huddle shields your flock before a big fight, and Ice Floe slows enemies on the exit route.

## puffin
Put Puffling turrets on the lane choke points and use Fog Bank to hide your own flock for a push. Sea Spray pushes dangerous melee birds back.


## peacock
Hold Hundred Eyes until a group is lined up, then dart the lot. Dazzle disarms, so use it on the bird about to shoot you. Open Full Display from behind cover: you are rooted while the fan opens.

## vulture
Drop Carrion Circle on a choke point and pick the bones of anyone who stumbles out of it. Thermal lifts an ally over a wall or out of trouble. Cast Death Spiral over a clump of enemies and fight on its rim.

## swift
Two Slipstream charges make a fine escape, so keep one back. Throw Wing Scythe down a lane and let it return through the same enemies. Sky Stitch gives free flight and a stronger gun, so use it to chase a wounded target.

## hummingbird
Dart in, tap the gun, Dart out. Nectar Sip heals you as it hurts, so use it on a bird that cannot hit back at close range. Blur makes you hard to kill for a moment, and Thousand Beats turns every Dart into a blade.

## cassowary
Aim Rampage at the enemy carry and let the knock-up set up your flock. Dagger Kick pushes a diver away and weakens their armour. Bristle before you step into the fray, then use Boom Call to drag the whole fight to you.

## toucan
Lob Fruit onto the ground enemies must cross, and use Seed Spit to reveal a target for your flock. Branch Swing is a way up and out, not just in. Save Fruit Barrage for a nest or a pinned group.

## woodpecker
Drumroll before the fight, not during it. Bore Hole weakens the armour of the first hero hit, so shoot that bird next. Cling to a tree for a safe, stronger gun, and let Hammerhead detonate on your focus target.

## nightjar
Stay invisible with Nightfall until you are in range, then open with the empowered hit. Gape heals you as it bites, so use it on a bird that cannot easily escape. Churring Call reveals a group for your flock, and Night Swallow finishes a wounded hero.

## frigatebird
Plunder Shot strips an enemy's buffs and shields you, so use it on a bird that has just cast one. High Pass lengthens your gun's reach, so fire from further back. Balloon Boom pushes off a diver, and Harpoon Line pulls a target into your flock.

## cockatoo
Screech silences a whole cone, so use it on the casters about to act. Crest Flare before a combo shortens your other cooldowns. Seed Crack lands late, so aim where enemies are heading. Channel Sulphur Shriek only when you are protected.

## swan
Grace Link heals you both, so link the ally in front. Hiss shoves away and slows anyone who gets too close. Lake Glide frees the flock from slows when a fight goes badly. Time Swan Song just before a big burst of damage.

## kiwi
Burrow out of danger and resurface under an enemy. Night Sense shows which enemy heroes are wounded. Probe weakens armour, so open with it. Ambush Pit is a finisher: tunnel to a squishy bird and erupt.
