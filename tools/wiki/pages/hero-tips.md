# Hero tips

## shrike
Lay the Thorn Larder where enemies will walk, not where they stand. Combine the Briar Patch with a root to hold a lane. Barbs travel, so lead moving targets.

## albatross
Open fights with the Wing Slam or Tempest Dive, then hold the Gale Shield between your flock and the enemy guns. The shotgun is strongest up close.

## kingfisher
Use Glint to start fights unseen, then spend your gun on whoever the ambush bonus hit. Riverstrike is a finisher, not an escape.

## lyrebird
Keep Mended Song for the ally taking the most damage. Echo Double draws shots away from you. Cast Discord on a group of enemy shooters, then back off.

## owl
Your range is your safety. Use Night Eyes to find the enemy flock and Hush to stop an enemy ability before it lands. Aim carefully: the Owl's head shots are the strongest in the game.

## pelican
Gulp a fledgling to refill health, or a hero to remove them from the fight. Switch on Feeding Frenzy as your gun starts running dry and fire in short bursts at range.

## secretarybird
Kick, dash and stamp in sequence. Each move feeds the next, so chain them rather than using them alone. Keep a stamina pip for escaping.

## starling
Fire Murmuration down a lane to hit a whole group. Scatter turns you into a swarm that cannot be shot, which makes it a fine escape and an even better entrance.

## hornbill
Mark your target with Marked Prey and focus them. Heavy Rounds slow, so open with them when you want a kill. Bounding Hop refills half the magazine mid-fight.

## flamingo
Place the Pink Pool where your flock will stand, not where it is now. Poise protects a carry from a stun, so cast it on the bird the enemy will go for.

## penguin
Toboggan bowls through groups and lets you cross open ground fast. Huddle shields your flock before a big fight, and Ice Floe slows enemies on the exit route.

## puffin
Put Puffling turrets on the lane choke points and use Fog Bank to hide your own flock for a push. Sea Spray pushes dangerous melee birds back.
