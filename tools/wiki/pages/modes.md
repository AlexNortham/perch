# Game modes

## Play: a 6v6 match

Choose **Play** on the main menu, then a bird. You are one of six birds on the Dawn flock, in your base on the full map. The other eleven places are [[units/bots|bots]]: five allies and six enemies, each a different bird. Fledgling waves run down all three lanes, and the neutral objectives (Thickets, Nests, runes, the Golden Egg and the Roc) are in play. Earn Seeds, level up, buy items, take down towers and break the enemy Patron to win. You start with 600 Seeds.

You can concede from 15:00 in the pause menu. Bot matches end: in a test of 20 seeded all-bot matches every one finished with a winner, in about 35 to 55 minutes.

## Practice Hub

Choose **Practice Hub** on the main menu for a small training arena with an enemy dummy and an ally dummy. The enemy dummy takes damage and resets; the ally dummy starts wounded so you can test healing. You start with a pool of Ability Points to try upgrades, and every ability is unlocked. **Swap bird** (in the pause menu and as a button on screen) opens the bird picker without leaving the hub: you respawn as the new bird with a fresh pool of Ability Points and the dummies reset. See [[units/practice-dummies|Practice dummies]].
