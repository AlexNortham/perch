# Game modes

## Practice match (Play)

Choose **Play** on the main menu, then a bird. You are the only hero on the Dawn flock, in your base on the full map, with fledgling waves on all three lanes and the siege structures in place. It is a real match against the structures: earn Seeds, level up, buy items and take down towers until you break the enemy Patron. Hostile hero bots come later.

## Practice Hub

Choose **Practice Hub** on the main menu for a small training arena with an enemy dummy and an ally dummy. The enemy dummy takes damage and resets; the ally dummy starts wounded so you can test healing. You start with a pool of Ability Points to try upgrades. See [[units/practice-dummies|Practice dummies]].
