# Units and objectives

A match is a siege, with prizes in between. Waves of fledglings feed the lanes, towers defend each flock's approach, and the Patron in each base ends the match when it falls. Between the lanes, the jungle holds camps, Nests and timed objectives, and eleven bots play the other birds.
