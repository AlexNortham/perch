    Crouch, slide and Ctrl

    Crouching while moving starts a slide. In Chrome and Edge, entering a match also enters fullscreen and locks the keyboard, which lets the game use Ctrl as the crouch key even while you hold W: normally a browser would close the tab on Ctrl+W. In Firefox the keyboard lock does not exist, so C is the crouch key there. Esc pauses the game; hold Esc to leave fullscreen (a browser rule).

    Options notes which key combinations only work in fullscreen on Chrome and Edge.

    Mouse and melee

    • Left mouse fires; hold to keep firing.
    • Right mouse aims, which zooms the view and tightens the spread.
    • Tap the melee key for a light swing, hold it for a heavy one. The parry key blocks a melee hit and stuns the attacker. See Combat.
    • Middle mouse pings: a tap is a contextual ping and holding opens a wheel.
