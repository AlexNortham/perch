    Game modes

    Practice match (Play)

    Choose Play on the main menu, then a bird. You are the only hero on the Dawn flock, in your base on the full map, with fledgling waves on all three lanes and the siege structures in place. It is a real match against the structures: earn Seeds, level up, buy items and take down towers until you break the enemy Patron. Hostile hero bots come later.

    Practice Hub

    Choose Practice Hub on the main menu for a small training arena with an enemy dummy and an ally dummy. The enemy dummy takes damage and resets; the ally dummy starts wounded so you can test healing. You start with a pool of Ability Points to try upgrades. See Practice dummies.

    Planned

    • Bot matches: you plus eleven bots in a six-versus-six match with any bird. Coming in Stage 4.
    • Neutral objectives: the Golden Egg, the Roc and Phoenix Ember, Thickets, Nests and Feather runes. See Planned objectives. Coming in Stage 4.
