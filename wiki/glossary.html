    Glossary

    Words you will meet in PERCH. 6v6 matches, two flocks, one Patron each.

    TermMeaning
    Dawn FlockThe south team (base at z = −150). Warm amber colours. Patron: the Elder Raven. See Old Mossmarket.
    Dusk FlockThe north team (base at z = +150). Cool violet-blue colours. Patron: the Great Owl. See Old Mossmarket.
    FlockA team of six heroes.
    PatronEach flock's giant guardian bird in the heart of its base. Kill the enemy Patron to win. Two phases. See Patrons.
    Elder RavenDawn Flock's Patron.
    Great OwlDusk Flock's Patron.
    Home RoostA flock's spawn platform behind its Patron. Heals allies, hurts enemies, contains a Magpie's Hoard. See Patrons.
    LaneOne of three authored paths between the bases that fledglings march along. See Old Mossmarket.
    HedgerowThe west lane: walled gardens, hedges, terraced houses with balconies. Short sightlines.
    RiverbankThe middle lane: a river with boardwalks, mills and the Old Mill Bridge over the Hollow. Long sightlines.
    PinewoodThe east lane: tall pines, timber houses on stilts, rope walkways. Strong high ground.
    JungleThe city blocks and woods between lanes, holding Thickets, Nests, Chimneys and Vines.
    FledglingsLane units that spawn in waves and march down every lane. Melee Scrappers and ranged Slingers. See Fledglings.
    SeedsThe single resource. Earned seeds raise your level; unspent seeds buy items. See Economy.
    Net worthTotal seeds a hero has earned in the match (spent or not). Sets level and bounties.
    Seed podThe glowing pod that pops out of a fledgling when it dies. Shoot it at the right moment to secure or deny it. See Economy.
    Last hitThe killing blow on a fledgling or neutral. A hero's last hit pays the instant share.
    SecureShooting a pod from a fledgling your side killed: pays you the pod share.
    DenyShooting a pod from your own side's dead fledgling before the enemy secures it.
    Sentinel HawkFirst lane tower (one per lane per flock). Punishes heroes who attack heroes under it. See Towers.
    Stilt HeronSecond, larger lane tower: stomp and beam. Its first kill unlocks a flex slot for the killers. See Towers.
    Nest WardenBase gate towers, two per lane per flock. See Towers.
    Roost ShrineTwo per flock inside the base. Both must fall before the Patron can be damaged. See Towers.
    Brood WardBackdoor protection: structures take −65% damage while no enemy fledgling is within 30 m. See Patrons.
    ThicketA jungle camp of neutral creatures (tier 1, 2 or 3) that pays seeds and respawns. See Planned objectives.
    NestA breakable woven nest (crate) in the jungle and on rooftops. Small seed payout, sometimes a crumb buff.
    Crumb buffA 60 s minor buff that sometimes drops from a Nest.
    Feather runeA timed pickup on the two rune bridges that gives a 90 s buff (Swift, Fierce, Sage or Stout). See Planned objectives.
    Golden EggObjective from 10:00. Carry it to the Hatching Stone to pay your flock. The carrier can't shoot or cast and is revealed. See Planned objectives.
    Egg Perch / Hatching StoneThe two stone circles on the centre line. The Egg spawns on one perch; the other is the Hatching Stone.
    The HollowThe sunken pit under the Old Mill Bridge in the map centre where the Roc lives.
    The RocNeutral giant bird boss in the Hollow, from 20:00. Drops the Phoenix Ember. See Planned objectives.
    Phoenix EmberDropped by the Roc. Claimed by landing a charged heavy melee on it. Gives the claiming flock a 180 s buff.
    Ember buffThe flock-wide buff from the Ember: faster respawns, stronger fledglings, +15% damage.
    The Magpie's HoardThe shop. Three columns (Talon, Plumage, Song) by four tiers. At the Home Roost and at lane Magpie stalls. See The Magpie's Hoard.
    Magpie stallA lane shop: one per lane per flock, between the Hawk and the Heron.
    Talon(1) Item category for gun power. (2) Damage type of guns and melee.
    PlumageItem category for health, resists, regeneration and movement.
    Song(1) Item category for ability power. (2) Damage type of abilities and most item effects.
    Song power (SP)Stat that scales ability effects: each effect lists "+k × SP".
    Talon resist / Song resistPercentage reduction of incoming Talon / Song damage.
    Level1–30, set by net worth. Each level-up is a Molt. See Economy.
    MoltA level-up: base stat growth (health, gun damage, SP, melee) and usually Ability Points. See Economy.
    APAbility Points. Spent on ability upgrade tiers costing 1, 2 and 5 AP. See Economy.
    Upgrade tierOne of three improvements per ability (T1 = 1 AP, T2 = 2 AP, T3 = 5 AP), bought in order.
    UltimateAbility slot 4. Unlocks at level 6.
    KitA hero's gun plus four abilities.
    Item slotHolds one item. 12 base slots.
    Flex slotAn extra item slot unlocked for the whole flock by objectives (4 in total). See Economy.
    ActiveAn item with a castable effect, bound to Z / X / N / V.
    Stamina pipOne charge of stamina. Traversal can spend whole or half pips; stamina regenerates over time.
    DashShort burst of speed on Shift; costs one pip.
    Dash-jumpJumping during the ground dash timing window for a long, low leap at the cost of a second pip.
    SlideCrouching while running: a fast, low slide that carries momentum. See Movement.
    MantlePull-up onto a ledge within reach while holding Jump. See Movement.
    UpdraftA rideable wind current strung above each lane, base to base (zipline). Boost and dismount at will. See Movement.
    Updraft stationA marked mount point on an Updraft (gates, near Herons and Hawks, and centre).
    ChimneyA vertical warm-air shaft that lifts you to a rooftop.
    VineA climbable vine or rope on a wall or tree.
    Beak shotA gun hit on a hero's head hitbox. Deals critical damage (×1.65 by default). See Combat.
    FalloffGun damage reduction with distance, between a start and end range.
    ParryA short block window (F) that stuns an enemy whose melee lands in it. See Combat.
    Heavy meleeCharged melee (hold Q): slower, stronger, lunges, can be parried. Claims the Phoenix Ember.
    BountySeeds paid for killing a hero, raised for shut-downs and for comeback kills. See Economy.
    Comeback bonusBounty and Egg multiplier paid to the flock with the lower total net worth. See Economy.
    PingA map marker placed with middle mouse (tap: contextual; hold: wheel).
    StatusA timed condition such as stun or slow. Full list in §5.9. See Statuses.
    Effect primitiveOne of the small set of building blocks every ability and item active is made from (§9.25).
