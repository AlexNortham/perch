    Cockatoo Coming in Stage 4

    Cockatoo

    Archetype
    Song burst
    Status
    Coming in Stage 4

    Song burst · Coming in Stage 4

    Appearance: stocky white body and a thick hooked grey bill, crowned by a tall sulphur-yellow crest that lies flat when idle and fans straight up (doubling the bird's height) when it casts or shrieks. Reads as "white bird with a yellow flame on its head".

    Coming in Stage 4. Everything below is the design plan from the Game Director's design document, and can change before Cockatoo is built.

    Plan

    height 1.75 m (raised crest to 2.1 m, no hitbox) · head r 0.18 m at 1.52 m · capsule r 0.34 m.

    HP 560 (+32/level) · move 7.0 m/s · stamina 3.

    Gun — Screech Horn: projectile 75 m/s · 14 Talon × 1 · spread 1.0° · falloff 14–32 m · 4.5 shots/s · magazine 20 · reload 2.0 s (63 DPS at level 1).

    Song-burst birds deal their damage through abilities. Spend Seeds on the Song column, line up your abilities so they land together, and keep your cooldowns rolling rather than hoarding them.

    Planned abilities

    Ability 1: Screech

    Enemies in the cone take 100 + 1.0×SP Song damage and are silenced 0.75 s.

    directionCooldown 11 sRange 14 m cone (45°)

    • Tier 2 · 2 AP−3 s CD
    • Tier 3 · 5 APSilence 1.5 s

    For 6 s: +25 SP and +15% Song damage. On cast, your other abilities' cooldowns drop by 3 s.

    selfCooldown 20 s
    • Tier 1 · 1 AP+2 s duration
    • Tier 2 · 2 AP+10 SP
    • Tier 3 · 5 APCooldown drop 6 s

    Ability 3: Seed Crack

    A nut lands and cracks 0.8 s later: 5 m radius, 120 + 1.1×SP Song damage, knock-up 0.5 s.

    pointCooldown 13 sRange 25 m
    • Tier 1 · 1 AP+1 m radius

    Ultimate: Sulphur Shriek

    Channel 2 s (50% move speed, no gun): 4 pulses, one every 0.5 s, each dealing 80 + 0.7×SP Song damage to enemies within 15 m. An enemy hit by 3 pulses is stunned 1.0 s.
