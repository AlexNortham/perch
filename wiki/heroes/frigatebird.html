    Frigatebird Coming in Stage 4

    Frigatebird

    Archetype
    Long-range poke
    Status
    Coming in Stage 4

    Long-range poke · Coming in Stage 4

    Appearance: angular and black, with very long narrow wings always held half-open in a sharp "W" (3.6 m span), a deeply forked scissor tail, a hooked bill and a big inflated red throat balloon on the chest. Reads as "black W with a red balloon".

    Coming in Stage 4. Everything below is the design plan from the Game Director's design document, and can change before Frigatebird is built.

    Plan

    height 1.8 m (the wings have no hitbox) · head r 0.15 m at 1.62 m · capsule r 0.30 m.

    HP 530 (+31/level) · move 7.0 m/s · stamina 3.

    Gun — Kite Rifle: hitscan · 40 Talon × 1 · spread 0.2° · falloff 35–80 m · 1.8 shots/s · magazine 8 · reload 2.5 s (72 DPS at level 1).

    Long-range birds win before the fight starts. Chip health from far away, take the high ground, and fall back when an enemy closes the distance.

    Planned abilities

    Ability 1: Plunder Shot

    Projectile 80 m/s: the first enemy hero hit takes 70 + 0.7×SP Song damage and loses all its positive statuses; you get a Song shield of 100 + 1.0×SP for 5 s.

    directionCooldown 14 sRange 50 m
    • Tier 1 · 1 AP+25 damage
    • Tier 2 · 2 AP−3 s CD
    • Tier 3 · 5 AP2 charges

    Ability 2: High Pass

    Leap in a 0.8 s arc (12 m apex) to the point; for 5 s your gun's falloff ranges are +40%.

    pointCooldown 16 sRange 30 m
    • Tier 1 · 1 AP−4 s CD
    • Tier 2 · 2 AP+10 m range
    • Tier 3 · 5 APThe bonus also gives +20% gun damage

    Ability 3: Balloon Boom

    The throat balloon bursts: enemies within 5 m take 50 + 0.5×SP Song damage and are knocked back 7 m; you get a barrier of 150 + 1.5×SP for 3 s.

    selfCooldown 15 sRange 5 m radius
    • Tier 1 · 1 AP+2 m radius
    • Tier 2 · 2 AP−4 s CD
    • Tier 3 · 5 APEnemies hit are also slowed 40% for 2 s

    Ultimate: Harpoon Line

    A hooked line flies at 120 m/s: the first enemy hero hit takes 200 + 1.5×SP Song damage, is pulled 15 m toward you over 0.5 s and is stunned 1.0 s.

    directionCooldown 90 sRange 70 m
