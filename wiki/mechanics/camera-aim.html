    Camera and aim

    PERCH uses an over-the-shoulder third-person camera. The crosshair is honest: the game casts a ray from the camera through the crosshair, and your shots, aimed abilities and unit-targeted abilities all start from that ray, so what you aim at is what you hit.

    Shoulder camera

    The camera sits behind your bird and to one side. Swap shoulders to look round a corner. When a wall would block the view the camera pulls in towards you.

    Aiming

    Hold the aim button (right mouse) to zoom and to tighten your gun's spread. Heroes with long-range guns, such as the Owl, zoom further. Your bird's gun fires from its muzzle toward the point the crosshair ray hits, so close-up shots can look off-centre but still land where you aimed.

    Unit targeting

    Abilities that lock onto a unit (a heal on an ally, a stab on an enemy) choose whoever is nearest the crosshair inside a narrow cone and within range. The cast fails if there is nobody there.

    Pings

    A middle-mouse tap places a contextual ping; holding opens a wheel. The number of pings is limited to stop spam.
