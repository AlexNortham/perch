    Economy

    Seeds are the only resource. Every Seed you earn adds to your net worth, which never goes down and sets your level, and to your wallet, which buys items.

    Seed pods

    When a fledgling dies, a seed pod pops out. Shooting the pod while it is ripe resolves it. A hero on the side that killed the fledgling secures it for the full pod share. A hero on the fledgling's own side can deny it: a smaller reward, and the enemy gets nothing. Abilities do not resolve pods; guns and melee do. A pod that is not hit withers.

    Molts

    Every Molt raises the bird's base stats: health, gun damage and Song power, with each bird's own growth listed on its hero page. A level-up also heals you by the health you gained.

    Ability Points

    Ability Points (AP) buy ability upgrade tiers, not items. Each ability has three tiers bought in order, anywhere and at any time, even while dead. Unspent AP carries over. Hold the upgrade key (Left Alt by default) to free the mouse, then click an ability icon or press 1 to 4; see the Abilities list.

    Bounties

    Killing a hero pays a bounty shared by the killer and the assisters. It grows with the victim's net worth and their kill streak, and gets a comeback multiplier when the victim's flock is richer than yours.

    Jungle rewards

    Seeds also come from the jungle: Thicket creatures, Nest sprigs, Bell Feeders, claiming a Phoenix Ember, killing the Roc, and a delivered Golden Egg. Each grows with match time, and the numbers are on Objectives and the jungle.

    Flex slots

    The first kills of each structure type also unlock flex slots: extra item slots shared by the whole flock for the rest of the match, shown in the last column above. See The Magpie's Hoard.

    Respawn

    Heroes respawn at the Home Roost with full health. Cooldowns keep running while you are dead.

    Respawn time is the smaller of 55 and 8 + 1.2 × minutes, rounded to 0.1 s. The Phoenix Ember buff multiplies it by 0.5.
