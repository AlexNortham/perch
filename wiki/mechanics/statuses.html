    Statuses

    A status is a timed condition on a bird, such as a stun or a slow. Harmful statuses last shorter on birds with status resist. On/off statuses (stun, silence, disarm, root and similar) keep one instance, and a new application extends it. Graded statuses (slow, damage amp, resist shred) keep each application separate: slows and heal reductions use only the strongest, while amps and shreds add together up to a cap. Damage-over-time statuses keep one instance per source.

    Displacement

    Knockback, knock-up and pull are impulses rather than timed statuses. Knockback and pull remove most of your air control until you land; a knock-up leaves you airborne and helpless until you land. Unstoppable removes them.

    Abilities that apply statuses list them as chips on each ability.
