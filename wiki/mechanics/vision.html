    Vision

    Your flock shares what its members, fledglings and structures can see. An enemy outside every sight radius is hidden from you: it vanishes from the world and from the minimap.

    SourceSees up to
    Hero40 m
    Fledgling20 m
    Structure30 m
    Invisible enemies are spotted within8 m
    True sight (default)30 m

    Revealing and hiding

    • Invisible birds are hidden even inside sight radius, except within a short range where a faint shimmer shows. True sight shows them fully.
    • Revealed birds appear on the enemy minimap and are outlined through walls.
    • Enemies in sight are outlined in red so they stand out from your flock, which is shown in team colours (see the map).

    See Statuses for the reveal, true sight and invisibility rules, and the Owl for a bird built around them.
