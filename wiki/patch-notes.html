    One section per stage, newest first. Past stages are the notes written when each milestone passed.

    Stage 3 (M2, The loop): in progress

    This stage has not passed its gate yet, so this section is built from the game's current data rather than from the release notes.
    • 8 new birds: Hornbill, Flamingo, Penguin, Puffin, Owl, Pelican, Secretarybird, Starling.
    • The Magpie's Hoard with 64 items, and levels, Ability Points and Molts from Seeds. See the economy.
    • Towers and the two-phase Patrons, so a match can be won by hand.
    • A full HUD: minimap, scoreboard, big map, kill feed, damage numbers and sound.
    • The Practice Hub with damage and healing dummies.

    20 of 24 birds are playable.

    4 more birds are planned: Frigatebird, Cockatoo, Swan, Kiwi.

    Stage 2: M1 — One bird in a lane · reached 19/11/2026

    How to get in: run npm start, click Play, and pick one of the four birds whose cards say Pick (the other 20 say "Coming soon"). You start a practice match: you alone on the Dawn flock in the gold base, on the full map, with fledgling waves on all three lanes and all four of your abilities unlocked. To skip the menus, open http://localhost:4173/?scene=lane&hero=shrike (or albatross, kingfisher, lyrebird).

    What you can do:

    • Move like the full game: run, dash, dash-jump, slide, mantle onto ledges, climb Chimneys and Vines, and ride the Updraft cables strung above each lane (mount at a station with E, jump to drop off).
    • Shoot: each bird's own gun, with spread, falloff, reloads and reload-cancel. Hits on the head are beak shots: more damage and a gold hit marker.
    • Use all four abilities from the first second (levels arrive in Stage 3, so the practice match unlocks everything).
    • Fight fledglings: waves leave both bases every 30 seconds on all three lanes. Dawn and Dusk fledglings march, meet and fight each other, and they fight you.
    • Seed pods: when a fledgling dies, a seed pod pops up. Shoot an enemy fledgling's pod while it's ripe to secure its Seeds; shoot one of your own fledglings' pods to deny it to the other side. The HUD shows SECURED or DENIED, and your Seeds go up.
    • Options (from the main menu or the pause menu): rebind any key (a key already in use swaps with it; Esc cancels; reset one or all), mouse sensitivity, field of view, graphics quality, volumes. Settings survive closing the browser.

    Not in yet:

    • No enemy birds. Bots arrive in Stage 4; until then the practice match is you against fledglings.
    • No towers or bases to attack. Fledglings stop at the enemy base and wait; the Hawks, Herons, Wardens, Shrines and the Patron arrive in Stage 3.
    • Seeds don't buy anything yet: levels, ability upgrades (Alt+1–4 needs ability points from levels) and the shop arrive in Stage 3.
    • No sound (volumes are saved for when it arrives in Stage 3), no minimap, scoreboard or kill feed.
    • 20 birds are still "coming soon"; they arrive in batches of four through Stages 3 and 4.
    • Rough edges: fledglings can clip through walls when they chase off the lane; Lyrebird's Echo Double upgrades (two decoys, the burst) are untested in play.

    Next, Stage 3 (M2 — The loop): levels and ability points, the Magpie's Hoard shop, Sentinel Hawks, Stilt Herons, Nest Wardens, Roost Shrines and the two-phase Patron, so a match can be won by hand; a full HUD with minimap, kill feed and scoreboard; sound; and eight more birds.

    Stage 1: M0 — Foundations · reached 19/11/2026

    What you see: the main menu, floating over the real map, "Old Mossmarket": a city built inside an old forest, lit by a low sun with soft shadows, distance fog and a sky that warms toward the Dawn base and cools toward the Dusk base.

    What you can do:

    • Look at the whole map from above. With npm start running, open these in the browser:
    • http://localhost:4173/?scene=blockout&cam=overview — the map from behind the Dawn base.
    • http://localhost:4173/?scene=blockout&cam=top — straight down, like a minimap.
    • Add &quality=low or &quality=high to any address to compare the graphics presets.
    • Pick out the layout: the Dawn base (gold) in front and the Dusk base (violet) at the far end; the three lanes, Hedgerow, Riverbank and Pinewood; the Updraft cables strung above each lane; Chimneys and Vines on the buildings; shops; and placeholder markers where the Sentinel Hawks, Stilt Herons, Nest Wardens and Roost Shrines will stand.

    Not in yet:

    • Nothing to play. Play leads to an unfinished match screen with the camera parked inside your base, and there is no hero to control. Birds, controls and the HUD arrive in Stage 2.
    • Behind the scenes the systems exist but have nothing to drive them: movement with real physics (run, dash, slide, mantle, Updrafts, climbing), guns with beak shots and the full damage pipeline, melee and parry, status effects, and the ability framework. They are proven by about 300 automated tests and a deterministic headless simulation (npm run sim -- --seed 1 --minutes 3 --bots 12), not yet by anything you can steer.

    Next, Stage 2 (M1 — One bird in a lane): pick Shrike, Albatross, Kingfisher or Lyrebird, drop into a lane, run, dash, slide, mantle and ride Updrafts, shoot with beak shots, use four abilities, watch fledgling waves march and fight, and secure or deny seed pods.
