    Units and objectives

    A match is a siege. Waves of fledglings feed the lanes, towers defend each flock's approach, and the Patron in each base ends the match when it falls.

    Built

    • Fledglings: Scrappers and Slingers, the lane units.
    • Towers: Sentinel Hawk, Stilt Heron, Nest Warden and Roost Shrine.
    • Patrons: the Elder Raven and the Great Owl, the Brood Ward and the Home Roost.
    • Practice dummies in the Practice Hub.
