    Nests

    • 80 HP; any damage breaks them (melee breaks one in two light hits).
    • Pay 30 seeds (+3% per minute of match time) to the breaker.
    • 25% chance (seeded) to drop a crumb buff for 60 s: Quick crumb +15% fire rate, Light crumb +1 m/s move speed, or Sweet crumb +15 SP (equal chances). A new crumb replaces an older one.
    • Respawn 4:00 after breaking. 32 on the map (§6.5).
