    • Resists: tiers 1 and 2 have 30% Talon and 25% Song resist; tier 3 has 40% and 35%.
    • Scaling, fixed when the camp spawns: HP +2% and damage +0.5% per minute of match time. Seeds are paid at death: base × (1 + 0.015 × minutes).
    • Dormant: a camp sleeps until a hero comes within 30 m. A sleeping camp does nothing and costs no sim work. While it sleeps, damage from a hero more than 35 m away does nothing, so camps can't be sniped from a lane.
    • Aggro: the first damage to any creature wakes the whole camp. Each creature attacks the hero who dealt the camp the most damage in the last 3 s and needs line of sight to do so. Slams and puddles hit every hero inside them.
    • Leash: 18 m from the camp anchor; indoor camps also stop at their room's doors. A creature may chase 3 m beyond the leash while it still sees its target. Past that, or after 6 s without taking damage, it walks home and heals to full, and the camp sleeps again 5 s later.
    • Payout: a creature's seeds are split evenly between the hero who lands the killing blow and every allied hero who damaged it in the last 5 s, rounded down per hero. No pods drop from neutrals. This replaces the Stage 3 last-hitter rule.
    • Respawn: a camp respawns all at once, at the time above after its last creature dies. Leaving one creature alive stops a camp from respawning.
    • Fledglings and creatures ignore each other.
